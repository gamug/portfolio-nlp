## SPEC item 22

22. **A quarantined pair is not a rejected article.** `portfolio-data-mining` classes each (article, ticker) pair as `accept`, `reject` or `review`; `reject` and `review` are quarantined. That is a judgement about the company tag, made from the URL and the title, not about the article: the SOURCE holds 374,419 quarantined rows with a title and a body (against 98,462 `ok`), some of them financial news about another company (examples in `docs/db-topology.md`; how many is T-160). **Decided (owner, 2026-11-08, `PLAN.md` D36):** a properly fetched article (a title and a body) is preserved and stays eligible for the pipeline whatever its pair class; stored results of a quarantined pair are moved aside reversibly, never the article; sizes say which corpus they are of. The cut stays on the `ok` window and the preserved corpus joins after the delivery; `thin_content` rows are not part of it. Counted (T-159): 309,882 unique properly fetched bodies against 81,961 `ok` (227,921 are quarantined-only; 73,771 against 29,113 in the cut's window). Open: how much of the quarantined-only part is about a company (`TASKS.md` T-160) and its effect on D19. No FR is added until a behaviour ships.
